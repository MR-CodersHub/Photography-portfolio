The Beginning

Where It All Started

Lumina Studio was born from a simple yet profound realization: every moment deserves to be remembered, not just recorded. Founded in 2015, we started with a single camera, an unwavering vision, and a commitment to editorial excellence.

What began as a passion project in a small Brooklyn loft has evolved into an internationally recognized photography house, trusted by couples, brands, and visionaries who demand nothing less than perfection.

Our journey has taken us from intimate backyard ceremonies to grand destination weddings, from emerging startups to Fortune 500 brands, always maintaining our core philosophy: authenticity over artifice, emotion over perfection.

How We Work

The Lumina Process

01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin with a deep dive into your vision. Through detailed conversations, mood boards, and creative brainstorming, we align on style, expectations, and the story you want to tell.

02

Creative Planning

Our team crafts a detailed shot list, timeline, and creative direction. We scout locations, plan lighting, and prepare every detail to ensure flawless execution.

03

The Shoot

On the day, we work seamlessly, capturing candid moments and crafting editorial compositions. Our unobtrusive approach ensures you stay present while we preserve every precious second.

04

Post-Production Mastery

Every image is meticulously edited using our signature color grading and retouching techniques. We enhance, never alter—preserving authenticity while elevating artistry.

05

Delivery & Beyond

Receive your curated gallery within 4-6 weeks, complete with high-resolution files, print-ready formats, and optional premium albums. We're here for you long after delivery.
